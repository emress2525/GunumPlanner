Namaz V8 content-rights audit progress

Finding: repository-level licensing alone is not sufficient evidence that every named third-party text layer may be redistributed.
Required release gate:
- Every bundled text layer must record source id, license name, attribution, and explicit redistribution allowance.
- Translation and commentary layers remain excluded until layer-specific evidence is recorded.
- CI must fail if a restricted layer appears in the APK without matching evidence.
- UI must not claim a translation/commentary layer is available while its rights gate is closed.
